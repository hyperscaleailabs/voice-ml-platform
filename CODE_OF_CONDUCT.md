# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, available in full at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: everyone participating in this project — issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews — is expected to act in a way that makes the project
welcoming to people regardless of background or experience. Be direct about
code, considerate about people.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable: harassment of any kind, personal
or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained
disruption of discussion. The Covenant's text is the authority on this; the
list here is a summary, not a limit.

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreeing with a design, a measurement or a review is not a conduct problem —
it is the work. This repository develops hypothesis-first precisely so that
disagreements are settled by a falsifier rather than by seniority. Criticise the
claim, the method or the code; not the person who wrote it. A review that says
"this number has no source" is doing its job; one that says "you always do this"
is not.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers by opening a confidential
report through the repository's security advisory form, or by contacting a
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
consequences determined by other maintainers.
